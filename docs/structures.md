# Structures

Every structure TotalSegmentator's Apache-2.0 tasks can give, and what
open-anatomy does with it. Generated by `scripts/structures_doc.py`; do not
edit by hand.

Both tasks used are open: `total` and `head_glands_cavities` are on
TotalSegmentator's Apache-2.0 list ([Subtasks](https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator#subtasks)).

## Not available from open tasks

| Structures | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Heart chambers (left and right atria and ventricles, myocardium) | only in TotalSegmentator's licensed heartchambers_highres task; the open total task has the heart as one structure and the left atrial appendage. No open route is verified: one would be an nnU-Net model trained on a chamber-labelled CT dataset under CC BY or CC0, and no such dataset's licence has been checked yet |
| Forearm, wrist, hand, lower leg and foot bones | only in the licensed appendicular_bones task |
| Fat and muscle tissue layers | only in the licensed tissue_types task |
